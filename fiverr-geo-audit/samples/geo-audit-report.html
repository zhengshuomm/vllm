4 Prioritized Action Roadmap

P0 · Critical No Schema.org JSON-LD structured data detected Structured Data

Impact: Without JSON-LD, AI engines must guess your entity name, category, pricing, and official profiles from unstructured HTML.

Recommended Fix: Paste the ready-to-deploy schema-jsonld.html snippet into your homepage <head>.

P1 · High Missing FAQPage Schema markup Structured Data

Impact: Conversational queries in ChatGPT and Perplexity map directly to structured Question/Answer pairs.

Recommended Fix: Add a 5–8 question FAQ section backed by the included FAQPage JSON-LD block.

P1 · High Few or no conversational question-style headings Content Extractability

Impact: AI search queries are phrased as natural questions ("How does X compare to Y?", "What does X cost?").

Recommended Fix: Rewrite key H2/H3 headings as direct buyer questions with a 40–60 word direct answer immediately underneath.

P2 · Medium No explicit AI User-Agent directives in robots.txt AI Crawler Access

Impact: Relying only on wildcard rules leaves AI crawler permissions ambiguous when security teams or CDNs update rules.

Recommended Fix: Add explicit Allow blocks for GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, and Google-Extended.

P2 · Medium No /llms-full.txt deep context file AI Discovery

Impact: Technical or B2B buyers benefit when AI assistants can read consolidated product docs in one fetch.

Recommended Fix: Optionally publish /llms-full.txt consolidating key product specs, pricing, and FAQ answers in clean markdown.

P2 · Medium Limited concrete numbers or verifiable statistics in copy Content Extractability

Impact: GEO research shows LLMs strongly prefer citing pages that contain concrete figures, benchmarks, and transparent pricing.

Recommended Fix: Include concrete metrics (customer count, latency/speed savings, SLA numbers, or transparent pricing tiers).

P2 · Medium Missing freshness / authorship signals (dateModified / author) Content Extractability

Impact: AI engines prioritize recently updated and attributed content when choosing citations.

Recommended Fix: Expose `datePublished`, `dateModified`, and named expert authors on articles and comparison guides.
