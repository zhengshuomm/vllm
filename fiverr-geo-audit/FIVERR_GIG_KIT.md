## 四、FAQ（直接复制到 Fiverr FAQ 栏）

**Q1: Do you guarantee my brand will rank #1 on ChatGPT or Perplexity?**
> No—and you should avoid any seller who promises that. LLM answers are probabilistic and vary by user context. I deliver verifiable technical fixes (crawler access, `/llms.txt`, JSON-LD entity graph, SSR extractability) and a structured prompt benchmark so you can measurably improve citation readiness over time.

**Q2: Do you need login access to my website?**
> Not for Basic or Standard! Everything is delivered as a clear PDF/HTML report plus ready-to-upload files (`llms.txt`, `robots.txt` snippet, JSON-LD code) that you or your developer can deploy in under 15 minutes. If you want me to install them directly, message me for a custom offer.

**Q3: What platforms do you work with?**
> Any public website—Shopify, WordPress, Webflow, Next.js, Framer, Wix, or custom stacks.

**Q4: What is `/llms.txt` and why does it matter?**
> `/llms.txt` is a clean markdown manifest placed at the root of your domain that summarizes your brand, core products, pricing, and documentation in a format AI assistants can parse accurately without noisy HTML.

**Q5: How is GEO / AEO different from traditional SEO?**
> Traditional SEO focuses on ranking ten blue links on Google. GEO / AEO optimizes your site so AI retrieval engines can crawl your raw HTML (without JavaScript failure), verify your brand entity via Schema.org, and extract concise, factual answers to cite.
